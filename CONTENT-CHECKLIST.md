# School approval and integration checklist

The source proposal is a working draft, not verified school publication data.

- [x] Supplied crest preserved; motto and proposal palette used.
- [x] Public pages, navigation, portal handoff and responsive components implemented.
- [ ] School-approved welcome copy, mission, vision, values and headmaster message.
- [ ] Verified history, founding year, founder narrative and governance.
- [ ] Current subjects, examination bodies, A-Level combinations and departments.
- [ ] Admissions windows, fees, required documents, entry criteria and available places.
- [ ] Current boarding, welfare, sports, clubs and practical-learning details.
- [x] Postal address and role-labelled telephone numbers supplied for the Head, Deputy Head and Senior Master.
- [ ] Exact physical address, approved email, office hours and map coordinates.
- [ ] Six to twelve real school photographs with publication approval.
- [ ] Three approved news stories, verified achievements and confirmed events.
- [ ] Alumni profiles, projects and the appropriate contact route.
- [ ] Confirm results portal availability and school ownership of its supplied URL.
- [ ] Existing administrator source/API access to implement restricted content management, roles, drafts and audit trail.
- [ ] Approved enquiry destination and live delivery/retention setup.
- [ ] Official domain/DNS, public publishing approval, indexing, sitemap and structured data.

No optional online applications, student uploads or payment collection are implemented.
